## The 3-check ski day
1. Before the first lift: face covered, goggles meet the helmet, one dry sock, mittens on.
2. At every warm-up break: look at cheeks and noses, feel fingers and toes, swap anything wet.
3. After lunch: quiet, clumsy or cranky? Make it a shorter afternoon.

## Is it too cold today?
- At 5°F (temperature or wind chill) and colder, cut back time outside ([CPS](https://caringforkids.cps.ca/handouts/safety-and-injury-prevention/frostbite)).
- Below -15°F, avoid outdoor play; exposed skin begins to freeze within minutes ([AAP](https://www.healthychildren.org/english/safety-prevention/at-play/pages/winter-safety.aspx)).
- 0°F with a 15 mph wind feels like -19°F; frostbite can happen in about 30 minutes ([NWS](https://www.weather.gov/safety/cold-wind-chill-chart)).
- A body temperature below 95°F is a hypothermia emergency ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/winter-weather/prevention/index.html)).
