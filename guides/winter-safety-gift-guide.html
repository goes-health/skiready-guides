Winter Safety Gift Guide for Ski Families (2026)

The best gifts for a ski family keep a kid warm, covered and comfortable enough to want another run. Here's a short, practical list, plus one gift for the grown-up who hikes where signal drops.

Updated Nov 27, 2026 · Sources: REI Expert Advice, WinterKids, Sun & Ski, WMS, Smartwool

Gear for kidsFor the grown-upsSale ends Nov 30FAQ

Quick answer

  1. Warmth first: a wicking base layer (merino or synthetic, never cotton), real mittens and a neck gaiter.
  2. Head and eyes next: a snug helmet and goggles that sit flush against it.
  3. For the adults: give them a plan for when the signal drops, not just another gadget.

Parent FAQ

Can I give GOES+ as a gift?

There's no gift-subscription button, because GOES+ is bought in the App Store or Google Play by the person using it. Send an Apple or Google Play gift card, then help them download GOES and start the 7-day free trial.

Mittens or gloves for a young skier?

Mittens. They're warmer than gloves for young kids, and waterproof, insulated pairs with a hand-warmer pocket work best (REI).

When should I replace a kid's ski helmet?

After damage, after a hard fall even if no damage shows, or after about 5 years (WinterKids).

Is cotton OK under a ski suit?

Skip it. Cotton holds sweat and gets cold, so choose merino or a synthetic base layer (REI; Sun & Ski).
