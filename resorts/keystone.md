## Altitude first
The base is 9,280 ft. If you can, spend a night around Denver first; make day one short; watch for headache, nausea, poor appetite, fussiness or poor sleep in the first 24 hours ([Children's Colorado](https://www.childrenscolorado.org/just-ask-childrens/articles/altitude-sickness/), [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/high-altitude-travel-and-altitude-illness.html)).

## Cold bands for kids
- At 5°F or colder (temperature or wind chill): cut back time outside ([CPS](https://caringforkids.cps.ca/handouts/safety-and-injury-prevention/frostbite)).
- Below -15°F: avoid outdoor play ([AAP](https://www.healthychildren.org/english/safety-prevention/at-play/pages/winter-safety.aspx)).
