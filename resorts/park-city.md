## Cold bands for kids
- At 5°F or colder (temperature or wind chill): cut back time outside ([CPS](https://caringforkids.cps.ca/handouts/safety-and-injury-prevention/frostbite)).
- Below -15°F: avoid outdoor play ([AAP](https://www.healthychildren.org/english/safety-prevention/at-play/pages/winter-safety.aspx)).
